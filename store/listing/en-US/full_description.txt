A complete calculator in a single app, with three modes you switch with one tap:

• BASIC: the four operations, percent, parentheses, sign change, history and copy/paste of numbers.
• SCIENTIFIC: trigonometry in degrees or radians, logarithms, powers, roots of any index, factorial, constants, memory and previous result.
• FINANCIAL RPN: X, Y, Z and T stack, present value, future value, payment, number of periods and rate per period, cash flows, net present value (NPV), internal rate of return (IRR), amortization and rate conversion.

Built for real financial math:
• 34-digit precision with no floating point rounding surprises.
• A clear sign convention: cash in and cash out have opposite signs, and the app explains when that is not respected, without ever changing a sign for you.
• IRR warns when more than one solution exists instead of showing a misleading result.
• Amortization table, payments at the beginning or end of the period, and correct conversion between nominal, effective and equivalent rates.
• Unified history with filter by mode, detail, copy, reuse and delete.
• Financial registers are kept when you close the app, with an explicit, confirmed action to clear them.

Privacy first:
• 100% offline: no internet, no account, no ads, no purchases, no tracking.
• Only one permission: vibration (optional).

Light, dark, AMOLED and Material You themes, portrait and landscape, English and Brazilian Portuguese texts, and screen reader accessibility.
